## Design Context

### Users
Algerian patients (primary) booking doctor appointments, plus doctors and admins. Context: worried/anxious patients browsing on phones late at night; desktop users during the day. Job: find a trusted doctor, book an appointment, feel confident about the platform.

### Brand Personality
Warm, trustworthy, human. Voice: a modern, caring private clinic — calm reassurance, not cold clinical efficiency.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light theme (anxious patients at night; healthcare trust). Deep trustworthy blue leads (dominant), warm cream surfaces + a single coral accent add humanity. Tinted neutrals (no pure gray/black/white).
- Arabic-first typography: El Messiri (display, headings) + Readex Pro (body). Generous whitespace, dramatic type scale (3-5x jumps), asymmetric compositions, gentle entrance motion (ease-out, staggered), respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Flagship quality bar.
- Anti-references (AI-slop bans): NO gradient text, NO purple/blue gradients, NO glassmorphism, NO side-stripe borders on cards, NO neon-on-dark, NO rounded-rect-with-generic-shadow monotony.

### Design Principles
1. Warm over clinical — the interface feels like a caring practice, not a sterile portal.
2. Arabic-first elegance — Arabic is the typographic hero; RTL-aware composition everywhere.
3. Trust through depth — layered, elevated surfaces; soft but present shadows.
4. Calm, dignified motion — staggered reveals, exponential easing, reduced-motion respected.
5. Bold restraint — one dominant blue, one warm accent, dramatic scale, empty space that breathes.
